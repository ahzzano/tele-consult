# Product Deck Outline

Use this outline for the Product Manager track deck.

## Slide 1: Product Overview

Tele-Consult is a web-based telehealth MVP that helps patients find the right doctor, book a consultation, join online sessions, and receive medical records and prescriptions after care.

## Slide 2: Problem

Patients often struggle to choose the right doctor for symptoms, understand availability, and keep consultation records organized. Doctors need a lightweight way to manage availability and document outcomes.

## Slide 3: Users

- Patient: books and attends consultations, reviews records and prescriptions.
- Doctor: manages availability, conducts sessions, and records notes/prescriptions.

## Slide 4: Key Features

- Patient and doctor registration
- Profile management
- Doctor discovery and specialization filters
- Care Match symptom recommendation
- Online appointment booking, reschedule, and cancellation
- Doctor availability planning
- Real-time in-app notifications
- Consultation session links
- Medical records and prescriptions

## Slide 5: Patient Journey

Register, complete profile, describe symptoms, review recommended specialty, book an available doctor, join session, then view records and prescriptions.

## Slide 6: Doctor Journey

Register, create profile, set consultation hours, receive appointment notifications, join sessions, add notes, and issue prescriptions.

## Slide 7: Value Proposition

Tele-Consult reduces care-navigation friction by connecting symptoms, doctor discovery, scheduling, consultation, and post-visit records in one focused workflow.

## Slide 8: Differentiation

The MVP keeps care matching explainable, scheduling transparent, and doctor documentation tied directly to consultation history.

## Slide 9: Technical Approach

Next.js frontend, NestJS backend, PostgreSQL database, Docker Compose deployment, and modular backend domains.

## Slide 10: Limitations And Roadmap

Current limitations:

- Jitsi-based consultation sessions rather than custom video.
- Rules-based Care Match rather than full AI triage.
- In-app notifications rather than mobile push.

Roadmap:

- More complete prescription instructions.
- Persistent notification history.
- Clinical triage safety checks.
- Production deployment hardening.
